# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sicurre serves French auto-entrepreneurs and very small businesses who rely on email but do not have an IT or security team. They use the product between client work, invoicing, and operations, so the interface must be quick to understand, calm under pressure, and forgiving when something is misclassified.

## Product Purpose

Sicurre protects inbound email by routing messages through Cloudflare Email Routing, classifying them as phishing, spam, or legitimate, and giving users a clear place to review threats, quarantine decisions, domain setup, and feedback. Success means a non-technical professional can connect a domain, trust the protection state, correct mistakes, and report missed threats without learning security infrastructure.

## Brand Personality

Rassurant, simple, French. The product should feel like a careful professional service: precise, plain-spoken, and quietly protective rather than dramatic or fear-driven.

## Anti-references

Avoid fake enterprise social proof, invented usage numbers, cyberpunk security dashboards, generic AI gradient pages, neural-network visuals, fear-based alert copy, and overdecorated card grids. Sicurre should not look like a speculative enterprise SOC tool or a template SaaS landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Show the protection path clearly: domain, routing, classification, quarantine, feedback.
2. Earn trust with concrete product behavior, not fake testimonials or inflated metrics.
3. Keep French copy professional, direct, and reassuring.
4. Make every state actionable: connected, incomplete, empty, loading, failed, and corrected.
5. Treat dark mode as its own working environment with equal hierarchy and contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced-motion support, and copy that does not rely on color alone for verdicts or errors. French remains the default user-facing language, with English reserved for developer-only surfaces and technical identifiers.
